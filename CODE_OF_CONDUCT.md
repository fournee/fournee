# Code of conduct

Fournée follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and welcome people of every background and experience level. Criticise the
work, never the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated anywhere in the project: issues,
pull requests, discussions or any other space where you represent it.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, tell a maintainer privately through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/fournee/fournee/security/advisories/new) (choose any title and start
it with "Conduct"). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers will respond, and may warn, temporarily ban or
permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
